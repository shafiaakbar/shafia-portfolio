Part 2 of 3

Break it on purpose

Drag Background noise above 50% and call again. Watch which pedal goes wrong first, and what happens to the two after it.

That chain reaction is called the cascade. Pedals 2 and 3 can't hear the caller. They only get the note from pedal 1, so if the note is wrong they do a perfect job with the wrong information. That's why the best fix is usually at the start, in the listener.

Part 3 of 3

When are they done talking?

People pause mid-sentence all the time, and an AI has to decide when to jump in. Your turn: press Start, then tap Reply the moment the caller has finished.

Press Start to hear the caller.
talkingsilencebest time to reply

See the long gap after “um…”? A simple bot waits for a fixed silence, then talks. That's why bad bots interrupt you. Good agents add a 4th AI for turn detection: it reads the words and the tone to guess whether the sentence is really finished.
